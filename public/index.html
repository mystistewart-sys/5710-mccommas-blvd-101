The Property

A low-maintenance address that still puts you in the middle of East Dallas.

"Lock it, leave it, and let the neighborhood do the rest."

Unit 101 sits on the ground floor of Building 3 at Greenwood Flats, a stucco-and-wood three-story building completed in 2016 on McCommas Blvd in East Dallas.

Inside, 1,624 square feet runs on one level with no interior stairs. The living room measures roughly 28 by 13 feet and opens directly into the dining area and kitchen, with windows on more than one wall. Polished concrete floors carry through the main living spaces.

The layout is the part worth paying attention to. Both bedrooms have their own full bath, so neither one is the compromise bedroom. And the plan adds a separate 9-by-10 den — which means a home office, studio or guest space without surrendering the second bedroom. For a two-bedroom condo, that is unusually flexible.

What you do not get is a maintenance list. The $457 monthly HOA covers building insurance, structural maintenance and management, and two assigned covered spaces sit in the building's underground garage. There is no roof to replace, no yard to mow, and no exterior to repaint.

The location

Between Lower Greenville and the M Streets.

McCommas Blvd runs through East Dallas just south of Mockingbird Lane, a short turn off Greenville Avenue and minutes from Central Expressway.

That puts the building inside the stretch of East Dallas that people move here for — the Greenville Avenue restaurant and patio corridor, the Granada Theater, and Mockingbird Station's shops, cinema and DART light rail platform. The M Streets and their Tudor-style blocks sit immediately alongside, SMU is just north in University Park, and White Rock Lake and its loop trail are east.
